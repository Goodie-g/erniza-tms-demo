About Us

100% Tanzanian owned. 100% committed to making a difference.

Our Story

Tanzanian Roots, African Heart

Erniza Africa Safaris was born from a simple but powerful belief: that tourism should uplift the people and places it touches. Founded in Arusha, Tanzania, by a passionate team of Tanzanian professionals, our company is 100% locally owned and operated. Every guide, every driver, every cook — they're all Tanzanian, sharing their homeland with pride and deep knowledge.

Unlike foreign-owned safari companies where profits leave Tanzania, every dollar you spend with Erniza stays in the country. We reinvest in our communities, support local businesses, and ensure that the benefits of tourism reach those who need it most.

Our Mission

To provide world-class safari experiences while channeling our profits into meaningful social impact. We believe that tourism can — and should — be a force for good. That's why a significant portion of our profits goes directly to supporting orphanages, children's charities, and community development projects across Tanzania.

What We Stand For

The principles that guide everything we do

100% Tanzanian

Proudly owned and operated by Tanzanians. Our team lives here, our families are here, and our commitment to this land is absolute and personal.

Profit With Purpose

A significant share of our profits supports orphanages, children's homes, and community charities. When you travel with us, you're directly helping Tanzanian children in need.

Sustainability

We're committed to responsible tourism that protects Tanzania's wildlife and ecosystems for generations to come. Eco-friendly camps, reduced plastic, and conservation support.

Community First

We employ locally, source locally, and invest locally. Our success is measured not just in profits, but in the number of families we support and communities we uplift.

Safety & Quality

Your safety and comfort are paramount. Our guides are certified Wilderness First Responders, our vehicles are meticulously maintained, and our camps meet the highest standards.

Authentic Experiences

No cookie-cutter tours. Every itinerary is crafted with local insight, taking you beyond the tourist trail to the real Tanzania we know and love.

Where Your Safari Makes a Difference

Every booking with Erniza Africa Safaris directly contributes to these causes

Children's Homes & Orphanages

We partner with orphanages in Arusha, Moshi, and Karatu, providing funding for food, education, clothing, and medical care. Your safari helps feed and educate orphaned and vulnerable children across Tanzania.

Education Support

We fund school supplies, uniforms, and scholarships for children from disadvantaged backgrounds. Education is the key to breaking the cycle of poverty, and we're committed to opening doors for Tanzania's next generation.

Healthcare Initiatives

We contribute to community health programs, including malaria prevention, maternal health services, and medical supplies for rural clinics that serve communities near our safari destinations.

Community Development

From clean water projects to women's empowerment initiatives, we invest in projects that create lasting positive change in the communities that welcome our guests.
