The Perfect Safari Complement

After the excitement of your safari, unwind on the pristine beaches of Zanzibar. This enchanting archipelago offers powdery white sand, crystal-clear turquoise waters, rich Swahili culture, and some of the best diving and snorkeling in the Indian Ocean. From exploring the winding alleys of historic Stone Town to swimming with dolphins off the coast, Zanzibar is the ultimate tropical escape.

Culture & History

Stone Town

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Stone Town is a fascinating maze of narrow streets, ornate wooden doors, bustling bazaars, and historic buildings reflecting Arab, Persian, Indian, and European influences.

Sultan's Palace Carved Doors Markets Street Food

Must-do: Sunset at Forodhani Gardens, Freddie Mercury's birthplace, Slave Market memorial
Best Beaches

Nungwi & Kendwa

Located on the northern tip of Zanzibar, these beaches offer powdery white sand and calm, swimmable waters year-round. Famous for spectacular sunsets and vibrant beach life.

White Sand Swimming Sunsets Beach Bars

Must-do: Dhow boat sunset cruise, visit the Mnarani turtle sanctuary
Diving & Snorkeling

Mnemba Atoll

A pristine marine reserve offering some of Africa's best snorkeling and diving. Swim alongside dolphins, sea turtles, and vibrant coral reefs teeming with tropical fish.

Coral Reefs Dolphins Sea Turtles Snorkeling

Must-do: Snorkeling trip, dolphin watching, dive the Mnemba wall

Things to Do in Zanzibar

Spice Tours

Visit aromatic spice farms and learn why Zanzibar is called the "Spice Island."

Dhow Cruises

Sail on a traditional wooden dhow at sunset with music and refreshments.

Dolphin Watching

Swim with wild dolphins in Kizimkazi in their natural habitat.

Jozani Forest

See the rare red colobus monkeys in Zanzibar's only national park.

Cooking Classes

Learn to prepare traditional Swahili dishes with local chefs.

Deep Sea Fishing

Try your luck catching marlin, tuna, and sailfish in the Indian Ocean.
