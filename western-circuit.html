Tanzania's Wild West

The Western Circuit is Tanzania's most remote and least-visited safari region — and that's exactly what makes it extraordinary. Here, in the far west of the country, you'll find vast floodplains teeming with hippos and buffaloes in Katavi, and the misty forests of Mahale Mountains where wild chimpanzees roam free just steps from the shores of Lake Tanganyika.

This is Africa at its most primal and untouched. With only a handful of visitors each year, the Western Circuit offers an exclusivity that's increasingly rare in today's world. From tracking chimpanzees through lush rainforest to watching massive hippo pods in shrinking dry-season pools, every moment here feels like a discovery.

Remote Wilderness

Katavi National Park

Tanzania's third-largest national park and one of its most untouched. During the dry season, the Katuma River and its floodplains host Tanzania's densest concentrations of hippos and crocodiles. Massive buffalo herds roam the plains, and lion prides follow them closely.

Mahale Mountains National Park

Home to one of Africa's largest populations of wild chimpanzees, Mahale is a truly magical destination. Trek through lush rainforest to spend time with habituated chimp troops, then relax on the pristine white sand beaches of Lake Tanganyika.

Why Choose the Western Circuit?

Chimpanzee Encounters

The Western Circuit is the only place in Tanzania where you can trek to see wild chimpanzees. Mahale offers world-class primate experiences that rival anything in Uganda or Rwanda.

True Exclusivity

With only a few hundred visitors per year, you'll often have entire landscapes to yourself. This is safari without the crowds — raw, personal, and deeply immersive.

Lake & Beach Combination

Nowhere else in Tanzania can you combine a traditional safari with beach relaxation on a pristine freshwater lake. Mahale's white sand beaches are among Africa's most beautiful.

Unique Wildlife Spectacles

Katavi's dry-season hippo congregations and massive buffalo herds are wildlife spectacles that rival the Serengeti migration — but with a tiny fraction of the visitors.

Diverse Landscapes

From vast floodplains to misty rainforest mountains, from savanna to pristine lake shores — the Western Circuit packs incredible landscape diversity into a compact region.

Venture Into the Wild West

The Western Circuit is for those who seek true adventure. Let us take you to Tanzania's most remote and rewarding wilderness.
