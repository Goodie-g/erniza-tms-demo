Africa's Best-Kept Secret

While the Northern Circuit draws the crowds, the Southern Circuit remains wonderfully wild and untouched. This is Tanzania at its most authentic — vast landscapes with few vehicles, intimate wildlife encounters, and a sense of true adventure.

Here you can take boat safaris through hippo-filled channels, walk through the bush with an armed ranger, and watch lions stalk their prey without another vehicle in sight. The Southern Circuit offers a completely different safari experience — raw, exclusive, and deeply personal.

Africa's Largest Reserve

Selous Game Reserve (Nyerere NP)

Now renamed Nyerere National Park, the Selous is Africa's largest protected wildlife area — bigger than Switzerland. The lifeblood of the reserve is the mighty Rufiji River, which creates a network of lakes and channels perfect for boat safaris.

10% of World's Lions

Ruaha National Park

Ruaha is the park that safari connoisseurs whisper about. Tanzania's largest national park, it's home to 10% of the world's lion population and boasts incredible predator densities. The Great Ruaha River acts as a lifeline, drawing massive herds during the dry season.

Easy Access from Dar

Mikumi National Park

Often compared to a mini-Serengeti, Mikumi offers excellent wildlife viewing just a few hours' drive from Dar es Salaam. The Mkata Floodplain provides classic savanna scenery with open plains where you can see lions, giraffes, zebras, and elephants against the backdrop of the Uluguru Mountains.

Why Choose the Southern Circuit?

Exclusivity

Escape the crowds. While the Serengeti can have dozens of vehicles at a sighting, in the South you'll often be the only ones there, creating truly intimate wildlife encounters.

Unique Activities

Boat safaris on the Rufiji River, walking safaris with armed rangers, and fly camping offer experiences simply not available on the Northern Circuit.

African Wild Dogs

The Southern Circuit offers some of the best chances in Africa to see the endangered African wild dog in its natural habitat — a highlight for any safari enthusiast.

Raw Wilderness

Experience Africa as it was meant to be — vast, untamed, and authentically wild. No mass tourism, no crowded viewpoints, just pure safari magic.

Incredible Photography

With fewer vehicles and patient guides, you'll have time to compose the perfect shot without rushing. The light and landscapes are spectacular.

Great Value

Often more affordable than the Northern Circuit while offering a more intimate and personalized experience with higher guide-to-guest ratios.
