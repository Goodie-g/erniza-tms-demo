Mount Meru

Arusha National Park, Tanzania | 4,566m / 14,980ft

Home / Mountains / Mount Meru

Tanzania's Hidden Gem

Often overshadowed by its famous neighbor Kilimanjaro, Mount Meru is a spectacular volcano that offers one of East Africa's most rewarding climbs. Located in the heart of Arusha National Park, this trek combines stunning scenery, diverse wildlife, and breathtaking views of Kilimanjaro from the summit.

At 4,566 meters, Mount Meru is Tanzania's second-highest peak and the fifth-highest in Africa. The climb follows the Momella Route, a well-maintained trail that takes you through lush forests, across alpine meadows, and up to the dramatic crater rim. What sets Meru apart is the incredible wildlife viewing on the lower slopes — you're likely to encounter giraffes, buffaloes, warthogs, and colobus monkeys during your trek.

Many climbers use Mount Meru as an acclimatization trek before attempting Kilimanjaro, but it's a magnificent standalone adventure. With a 98% summit success rate and only 3-4 days required, it's perfect for those with limited time or those seeking a less crowded mountain experience.

The Momella Route Itinerary

4-Day Itinerary (Recommended)

Day 1: Momella Gate to Miriakamba Hut

Your adventure begins at Momella Gate (1,500m) in Arusha National Park. After registration and meeting your armed ranger escort, you'll trek through lush montane forest. Keep your eyes peeled for giraffes, buffaloes, and the black-and-white colobus monkeys that call this forest home.

Elevation Gain: 1,014m Distance: 10km Time: 4-6 hours Miriakamba Hut: 2,514m

Habitat: Montane forest | Look for: Elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, colobus monkeys

Day 2: Miriakamba Hut to Saddle Hut

A steeper climb today as you leave the forest and enter the heath and moorland zone. The vegetation changes dramatically, and views of the Meru crater begin to emerge. In the afternoon, take an acclimatization hike to Little Meru (3,820m) for spectacular sunset views of Kilimanjaro.

Elevation Gain: 1,056m Distance: 8km Time: 4-5 hours Saddle Hut: 3,570m

Side trip: Little Meru (3,820m) for acclimatization | Habitat: Heath and moorland

Day 3: Saddle Hut to Socialist Peak to Miriakamba Hut

The big day! An alpine start at midnight. You'll navigate the narrow ridge to Rhino Point (3,800m) and continue along the dramatic crater rim to Socialist Peak (4,566m). From the summit, enjoy one of Africa's most spectacular views — Kilimanjaro rising majestically above the clouds. After celebrating, descend all the way back to Miriakamba Hut.

Summit: 4,566m Distance: 5km up / 13km down Time: 10-14 hours Miriakamba Hut: 2,514m

Summit departure: 12:00 AM - 1:00 AM | Highlights: Crater rim traverse, Kilimanjaro sunrise views

Day 4: Miriakamba Hut to Momella Gate

A leisurely descent through the forest, giving you one last chance to spot wildlife. At the gate, you'll receive your summit certificate before transferring back to Arusha. Celebrate your achievement!

Descent: 1,014m Distance: 10km Time: 3-4 hours Certificate: Yes!

3-Day Express Option

For experienced and well-acclimatized trekkers, we offer a condensed 3-day itinerary. Note: This option is more challenging due to limited acclimatization time.

Day 1: Momella Gate → Saddle Hut (skipping Miriakamba)

A long day combining Days 1 and 2 of the standard itinerary. Very demanding with significant elevation gain.

Gain: 2,070m Distance: 18km Time: 9-11 hours

Day 2: Summit Day → Descend to Miriakamba

Alpine start for summit, then descend to Miriakamba Hut.

Day 3: Miriakamba → Momella Gate

Final descent and certificate presentation.

Wildlife You May Encounter

Giraffes

Commonly seen on the lower slopes of the mountain within Arusha National Park.

Cape Buffalo

Large herds graze in the clearings. Your armed ranger ensures safe passage.

Colobus Monkeys

Black-and-white colobus monkeys are frequently spotted in the forest canopy.

What to Expect on Mount Meru

Armed Ranger Escort

Due to the presence of wildlife, all climbs include an armed park ranger for safety.

Hut Accommodation

Mount Meru has comfortable mountain huts at Miriakamba and Saddle with bunk beds and basic facilities.

Meals Included

All meals are provided during the trek, prepared by our mountain chef.

Safety Equipment

Pulse oximeters, first aid kits, and emergency oxygen are carried on all climbs.
