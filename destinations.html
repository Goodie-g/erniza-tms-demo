Western Circuit

Chimpanzees & Remote Wilderness

Tanzania's best-kept secret. The Western Circuit combines the wildlife-rich Katavi plains with chimpanzee trekking in the Mahale Mountains on the shores of Lake Tanganyika.

When to Visit Each Circuit

Northern Circuit

Best: June - October

Dry season, best wildlife viewing. Migration river crossings July-September.

Also good: January - March (calving season)

Southern Circuit

Best: June - October

Dry season for best game viewing. Green season (Jan-Mar) offers lush landscapes.

Accessible year-round

Western Circuit

Best: July - October

Dry season essential for Katavi. Chimpanzee trekking best in dry months.

Limited access Nov-May

Zanzibar

Best: June - October

Dry, sunny weather. Perfect beach conditions. Great visibility for diving.

Also good: December - February
