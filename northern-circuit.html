Africa's Most Famous Safari Destination

The Northern Circuit is the crown jewel of Tanzanian safaris. This legendary region encompasses the world-renowned Serengeti National Park, the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater, the elephant-rich Tarangire National Park, and the diverse Lake Manyara National Park. It's here that you'll witness the Great Migration, see the Big Five, and experience the Africa of your dreams.

Easily accessible from Arusha, the Northern Circuit offers something for every traveler — from luxury lodges to budget camping, from short 3-day trips to comprehensive 2-week adventures. With Erniza's expert local guides, you'll discover hidden corners and secret viewpoints that most visitors miss.

UNESCO World Heritage

Serengeti National Park

The name "Serengeti" comes from the Maasai word "siringet" meaning "endless plains." This vast ecosystem hosts the world's greatest wildlife spectacle — the Great Migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest and 250,000 zebras.

Ngorongoro Crater

The world's largest intact volcanic caldera, often called "Africa's Eden." This natural amphitheater is home to over 25,000 animals including the rare black rhinoceros. The crater floor offers some of the easiest Big Five viewing in Africa.

260 km² crater Black Rhinos 600m walls
Black Rhinos Lions Flamingos Hyenas Hippos Elephants Buffaloes
View Ngorongoro Safaris
Elephant Paradise

Tarangire National Park

Famous for its massive elephant herds and iconic baobab trees, Tarangire offers a more intimate safari experience. During the dry season (July-October), the Tarangire River becomes a magnet for wildlife, creating spectacular viewing opportunities.

2,850 km² Baobabs 3,000+ Elephants
Elephants Lions Leopards Giraffes Zebras Wildebeest Oryx
View Tarangire Safaris
Tree-Climbing Lions

Lake Manyara National Park

A gem of diverse ecosystems — from the alkaline lake hosting thousands of flamingos to the dense groundwater forest where tree-climbing lions lounge in acacia branches. A paradise for bird lovers with over 400 species recorded.

330 km² 400+ Bird Species Tree-Climbing Lions
