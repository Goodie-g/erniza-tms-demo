The oldest and most established route on Kilimanjaro, Marangu is the only route with dormitory-style hut accommodation. Known as the "Coca-Cola" route, it offers a relatively gentle gradient but has lower success rates due to shorter acclimatization time. Recommended with the 6-day option for better acclimatization.

The most popular route on Kilimanjaro, Machame offers stunning scenery through five diverse climatic zones. Known as the "Whiskey" route, it's more challenging than Marangu but provides better acclimatization with its "climb high, sleep low" profile. The 7-day option is highly recommended for best success rates.

Widely considered the most beautiful route on Kilimanjaro, Lemosho approaches from the west and offers panoramic vistas, abundant wildlife on the first day, and excellent acclimatization. The 8-day option provides the highest success rates. Perfect for photographers and those wanting a quieter experience.
